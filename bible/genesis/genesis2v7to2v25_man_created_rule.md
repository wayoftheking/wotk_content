# Genesis 2:7-2:25 - Man created to rule

## Notes

**2:7 - Nephesh**

Mankind carries the breath of life from God himself within him.
We were created in love - given the kiss of life
Nephesh - became a living soul - the word itself is like breathing in and out

**2:8-14 - God planted a garden...**

We possibly know where the garden was positioned: in north west Iran - near Lake Urmia.

**2:15**

Care / steward the garden:

Protect and safeguard her:

- Why did it need protection? From what?
- It needed protection from the devil and the other fallen angels
- The serpent attacked cunningly and not directly as it knew it would have lost in a fight as mankind had been given authority
  - The serpent cunningly ensured we lost our authority first so that it could attack the works of the Lord...

Your life has meaning and purpose:

- Called to be loved and love God
- Extend His rule and reign
- Work has meaning
- Called to build and nurture relationships



**2:16-17 - The Command**

There are boundaries to our autonomy and decision making
We were never called to carry the weight of deciding where those boundaries lie and what is correct or incorrect. We were called to stay in close intimacy with God and to learn His ways, His heart and to walk in them / put them into practice here on earth.

**2:18-25 - Not good to be alone**

We are not called to live alone.
A team not a hardened hierarchy.
