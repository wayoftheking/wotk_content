# Genesis 3 - Temptation and Fall

## Notes

**Nature of the curse:**

We see that man is cursed to till the soil and live from its fruit through the seat of his effort.
Life and work in the garden was blessed and without excessive toil.
In permaculture systems people have identified that tilling the soil is one of the worst ways to handle soil.
Amazing how God knew this all along and that part of the curse was found in the tilling the soil.
